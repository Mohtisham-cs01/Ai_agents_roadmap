Company Knowledge Base
Company Overview

Acme Technologies is a fictional software company founded in 2018. The company develops productivity software for small and medium-sized businesses.

The company headquarters are located in Berlin, Germany.

Acme Technologies currently has approximately 120 employees.

The company's main product is called AcmeFlow. AcmeFlow is a project management and collaboration platform designed to help teams organize tasks, manage projects, communicate with colleagues, and track deadlines.

AcmeFlow

AcmeFlow was launched in 2021.

The platform provides several features:

Project management
Task management
Team collaboration
File sharing
Deadline tracking
Activity notifications
Project dashboards

AcmeFlow supports teams ranging from 2 to 500 users.

The standard AcmeFlow plan costs €15 per user per month.

The enterprise plan is available for organizations with more than 500 users. Enterprise pricing is negotiated individually with the customer.

Customer Support

Acme Technologies provides customer support from Monday to Friday.

Support hours are 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time.

Customers can contact support through email or through the support portal.

The standard support response target is within 24 hours.

Enterprise customers receive priority support with a target response time of 4 hours.

Data Storage

AcmeFlow stores customer project data in encrypted databases.

Customer data is backed up every 24 hours.

Backups are retained for 30 days.

Acme Technologies uses servers located within the European Union for its European customers.

Security

Acme Technologies requires employees to use multi-factor authentication when accessing internal systems.

Customer connections to AcmeFlow are protected using HTTPS.

Passwords are stored using secure one-way hashing.

Security audits are performed once every year.

Company Vacation Policy

Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid vacation per calendar year.

Employees must request vacation at least two weeks in advance.

Vacation requests are submitted through the company's internal HR portal.

Unused vacation days can be carried over into the following year, but no more than 10 days can be carried over.

Office

The Berlin headquarters is open from 08:00 to 19:00 Monday through Friday.

The office is closed on weekends.

Employees can work remotely up to three days per week.

Product Roadmap

Acme Technologies plans to release a mobile version of AcmeFlow in 2027.
